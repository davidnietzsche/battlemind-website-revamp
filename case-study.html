01 · The challenge

Off the main street.
Lost in the crowd.

Yaki Soul entered a highly competitive KL nightlife and dining market dominated by established Changkat F&Bs. Despite a unique product, the outlet sat away from the main street: limited walk-in traffic, low brand awareness, and nothing to separate it from countless other izakayas in the city.

02 · The solution

Light it up.

01Seasonal and thematic campaigns that combined digital storytelling with on-ground activations.
02Campaign ecosystems built to drive weeknight traffic, late-night visits and repeat customers.
03Partnerships, community engagement, creator content and experiential marketing for continuous buzz.
