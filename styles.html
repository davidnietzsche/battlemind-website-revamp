How the two sides stay one family

Always shared

  • Battle logo and lockups, untouched
  • Oswald headlines, Inter body, DIN numbers
  • Brand red #D7000F as the action colour
  • The amber-red gradient as the signature

Battle Mind turns up

  • More gradient, more motion, rounder shapes
  • Cheeky copy, playful 3D icons
  • Cinematic moments (hero, About)

Battle X turns down

  • Gradient only as a line or progress bar
  • Square corners, grids, spec-sheet data
  • Calm motion: reveals and transitions only
